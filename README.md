A night-market satay stall game: take speech-bubble orders, skewer chicken, lamb, prawn or tofu, grill the skewers over charcoal (mind the hot centre slots!), flip each one once at the golden point, glaze the plate with peanut, spicy-sweet or soy, and serve before your customer's patience runs out. Perfect flips build your combo multiplier — survive from dusk to midnight and finish the night with the best grade you can earn.

## How to play

- Click a protein tray → skewer it into your hand (up to 4 at a time).
- Click a grill slot → place a skewer on the grill; click a grilling skewer to flip it (flip inside the golden window for a perfect flip); click a flipped skewer to take it onto the plate.
- Click a glaze bowl → glaze the plate (the plate needs skewers first, and only takes one glaze).
- Click a customer → serve them, if the plate exactly matches their order.
- Click the bin → discard the plate, or clear your hand's prep when the plate is empty.
